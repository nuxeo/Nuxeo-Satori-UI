# Security reviews

Every security review the business apps work needs, in one place: what each covers, where its
notes for the reviewer are, and the decision it rests on.

**Deferred until the app is finished.** Decided by the plan's author on 26 November 2026: the work
goes on without waiting for each review, and every review below is held when the app is finished,
with what it covers then. Until a review is done, nothing it covers is to be relied on outside a
development or demonstration server. Merging into the staging branch is not a review.

This page lists what is to be reviewed, not what a review found. Review packs, with what was found
and what was fixed while building, are kept outside this public repository.
