# Dialog

The dialog is the frame of a dialog that `MatDialog` opens: its title, its content and its row of
actions, laid out and spaced as Angular Material's dialog is, in your theme's colours. Use it at the
root of your own dialog component, so the component draws its words and its buttons and leaves the
frame to the library.

## Key features

- **A title that names the dialog.** `heading` is a level-two heading, and it is the dialog's
  accessible name: Material's title sets the dialog's `aria-labelledby`.
- **Content that scrolls.** What you put inside is the content. When it is taller than the dialog it
  scrolls, between a title and a row of actions that stay where they are.
- **Actions at the end.** What you mark `nxsDialogActions` is the row of actions under the content,
  at its end, in the order you write it.
- **The frame and nothing else.** Opening the dialog, its data, its focus and closing it stay
  `MatDialog`'s, as for any dialog component.

## Usage

<Source
  language="typescript"
  dark
  code={`import { NxsButtonComponent, NxsDialogComponent } from '@nuxeo/satori-platform/components';`}
/>

export const usageHtml = `<nxs-dialog [heading]="data.title">
  <p>{{ data.question }}</p>
  <ng-container nxsDialogActions>
    <nxs-button (press)="cancel()">{{ 'acme.cancel' | translate }}</nxs-button>
    <nxs-button appearance="filled" (press)="run()">{{ data.title }}</nxs-button>
  </ng-container>
</nxs-dialog>`;

<Source language="html" dark code={usageHtml} />

`heading` is required, and takes text you have already translated: the library ships no
translations for the text you give it. Open the component that draws it with `MatDialog.open`, and
close it through its `MatDialogRef`. It needs no providers, and has no registered ID: place it in a
template by its selector.

## Accessibility

- **Role and name.** `MatDialog` makes the dialog a `dialog`, and the title names it.
- **Reading order.** The title, then the content, then the actions, in the order they are written:
  the frame adds no element of its own between them.
- **Focus.** `MatDialog` moves the focus into the dialog when it opens, keeps it inside while it is
  open, and gives it back to what had it when it closes. The frame takes no focus itself.
- **Keyboard.** Escape closes the dialog, unless your component sets `disableClose`, as one does
  while its action runs.
