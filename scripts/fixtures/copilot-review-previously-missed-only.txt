`apps/​nuxeo-ui-e2e/​src/​search.spec.ts:237`

Unlike the page-provider branch, the NXQL branch never checks that the complete payload is present. When this fallback is exercised, replacing the payload with another zero-match term such as `zzz-no-such-document-zzz` can satisfy every assertion: HTTP 200, zero results, hygiene filters, and no injection markers outside literals. That does not prove the submitted term reached Nuxeo as data. Decode the captured literals independently of the production escaping helper and assert that one equals the payload, optionally followed by the search wildcard.
