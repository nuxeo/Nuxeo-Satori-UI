Nuxeo Satori — Beta

Acme Insurance builds its own UI.

Their application. Our platform package. One real Nuxeo server, with their real documents in it.

  • Their own shell, sign-in, browser, metadata view and search — no adf-hx, no Material
  • Built only on @nuxeo/satori-platform and its services
  • Then customised through the four layers, live, with no rebuild

What you just watched

Real data. Real customisation.

  • Every document, folder, metadata field and search hit came from Nuxeo on localhost:8080 — 11 seeded Acme documents in a repository of ~120
  • Layer 0 — a bootstrap fragment in their configuration package: rebranded and re-themed two ways on the same bytes, reload only
  • Layer 2 — provideAcmeExtensions(), one line in app.config.ts: a nav entry, a fail-closed rule and a panel resolved by ID
  • Layer 1 — a manifest fragment in their configuration package: renamed, reordered and hid navigation the build knows nothing about

Layer 3 (fork the template) is what the whole application on screen is. The Layer 1 manifest is fetched during startup, so it applies from the first load that has a session — an anonymous first load falls back to the packaged defaults, which the shell reports rather than hides.
